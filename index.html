DBSW

3D FORM-FINDING ENGINE — CLIENT-SIDE WASM

This tool generates 3D structural equilibrium forms (vaults, domes, cable-nets, tensile fabrics, and space trusses) using Dynamic Relaxation and Force Density methods solved entirely in your browser via WebAssembly — no data leaves your machine.

  • Multi-material support (Steel, Concrete, Masonry, Timber, Cables, Fabrics)
  • Point/line boundary restraints with reaction force vector outputs
  • Gravity, self-weight, and point load array processing
  • Tension vs. Compression axial force distribution maps

Author: Damian Brenlla — dbsw.uk
