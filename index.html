3D Form Finding Engine — Client-Side WASM

This tool generates 3D structural form-found geometries solved entirely in your browser via WebAssembly — no data leaves your machine.

  • Six structural material classifications (Steel Cables, Fabric Membranes, Concrete Shells, Timber Gridshells, Vaulted Masonry, Custom Isotropic)
  • Material-driven solver factory incorporating Force Density Method (FDM), Compression-Constrained Inverted Dynamic Relaxation, and Underwood DR
  • Boundary restraints defined exclusively via discrete spatial point nodes and 3D vector line supports
  • Interactive 3D viewport raycaster for graphical point load assignment along cable networks
  • Optional self-weight inclusion and configurable external point load arrays
  • Real-time stress/displacement maps with numerical reaction vectors and interactive 3D section cut-planes
  • Direct .STL export of solved 3D network geometry

This is a concept-stage design exploration tool, not a substitute for code-compliant structural verification of the final geometry.
