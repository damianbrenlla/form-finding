DBSW

3D Form-Finding Engine — Client-Side WASM

This tool generates 3D structural equilibrium forms (compression vaults, domes, cable nets, tensile fabrics, and space trusses) using Dynamic Relaxation and Force Density methods, solved entirely in your browser via WebAssembly — no data leaves your machine.

  • Multi-material support (Steel, Concrete, Timber, Masonry, Cables, Fabrics)
  • Configurable domain geometry, spatial grid density, supports, and prestress
  • Explicit support reaction force vectors (Rx, Ry, Rz in kN)
  • Tension vs. Compression axial force distribution visualization
  • Direct .STL geometry export capability

This is a concept-stage design exploration tool, not a substitute for code-compliant structural verification of the final geometry.
