3D Form Finding Engine — Client-Side WASM

This tool generates 3D structural form-found geometries, solved entirely in your browser via WebAssembly — no data leaves your machine. Algorithm strategy and morphological behavior adapt dynamically based on your chosen structural material.

  • Material classifications (High-Tensile Cables, Fabric Membranes, Concrete Shells, Timber Grid-Shells, Vaulted Masonry)
  • Discrete point and 3D vector line support restraints
  • Optional self-weight inclusion
  • Stress & displacement maps with interactive 3D section cut-planes
  • Direct .STL export of generated forms

This is a concept-stage design exploration tool, not a substitute for code-compliant structural verification of the final geometry.
